Every screen in Ouroboros sits in the same frame: the **sidebar** on the left, the
**header** across the top, and the screen you are on in between. Learn the frame once and
every task guide after this one reads the same way.

<Screenshot id="user-guide.shell" />

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the screens, in the order the work flows:

| Entry | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Mission Control — live loops, the queue, the build farm and what needs you. See [Mission Control dashboard](./dashboard.mdx). |
| **Issues** | Intake, sizing and queuing. See [Issues](./issues.mdx). |
| **Workflows** | The Workflow Studio. See [Workflows](./workflows/studio.mdx). |
| **Models** | Routing, the model registry and providers. See [Models](./models.mdx). |
| **Build Farm** | Runners and pools. The Build Analyzer opens from here. See [Build farm](../administration/build-farm.mdx) and [Build Analyzer](./analyzer.mdx). |
| **Knowledge** | Skills, learned facts and playbooks. See [Knowledge](./knowledge.mdx). |
| **Planning** | Roadmaps and drafted tickets. See [Planning](./planning.mdx). |
| **Research** | Not available yet — the row is marked **soon** and opens nothing. |
| **Insights** | How the loop is doing over time. See [Insights](./insights.mdx). |

At the foot, below a rule:

- **Needs You** — the [inbox](./inbox.mdx) of decisions waiting for a person. The number
  beside it is how many are open; it disappears when there are none.
- **Settings** — workspace settings: members, policies, integrations and more.

The entry for the screen you are on is lit. Some screens have no entry of their own — a
run's console, a pull request's verification page, a run's test results — and keep lit the
entry you opened them from: open a run from the dashboard and **Dashboard** stays lit, so
you know where you came back to.

**Collapse** the sidebar to a rail of icons with the chevron at its foot (**Collapse
sidebar** / **Expand sidebar**); hover an icon for its name. Ouroboros remembers the choice
in your browser. On a narrow screen the sidebar starts as the rail; on a phone-sized screen
it is hidden behind the **Open navigation** button in the header and closes when you pick
an entry or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## The header

From left to right:

- **The Ouroboros logo** — back to the dashboard.
- **The workspace chip** — *workspace* / *repository*, for example
  `acme-robotics / All repos`. Open it to:
  - **Switch workspace** — when you belong to more than one, pick another; the page
    reloads in it.
  - **Focus repository** — narrow the screens to one repository, or **All repos**. The
    focus is remembered per workspace in your browser.
  - **Workspace settings** — open Settings.
- **Search** — opens the [command palette](#the-command-palette).
- ***N* loops live** — how many loops are running right now.
- **Needs you · *N*** — runs that stopped for a person in the last seven days; it opens
  the [inbox](./inbox.mdx).
- **The bell** — not available yet: notifications arrive with a later release, and the bell
  does nothing today.
- **Your avatar** — the account menu: [appearance](#appearance), workspace switching,
  **Workspace settings**, **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Sign out**.

The live-loop and Needs-you pills are hidden while their count is zero.

## The command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> — <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac — anywhere, or
click **Search** in the header. Type part of a name to filter, move with <kbd>↑</kbd> and
<kbd>↓</kbd>, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to run the highlighted entry; <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a
click outside closes it.

<Screenshot id="user-guide.command-palette" />

The palette finds **screens and commands**:

- **Navigation** — **Go to Dashboard**, **Go to Issues**, **Go to Workflows**,
  **Go to Models**, **Go to Build Farm**, **Go to Knowledge**, **Go to Planning**,
  **Go to Insights**, **Go to Needs You** and **Go to Settings**. **Go to Research** is
  listed but greyed out until Research arrives.
- **Actions** — **Toggle theme** switches between light and dark, and **Sign out** signs
  you out.

Searching issues, runs and the queue from the palette is not available yet; use the
[Issues](./issues.mdx) screen and the [dashboard](./dashboard.mdx) for those.

## Appearance

The account menu — your avatar, top right — holds the two appearance settings:

<Screenshot id="user-guide.appearance" />

- **Theme** — **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System follows your operating system and
  changes when it does; it is the default. The choice is saved in this browser only, so
  another browser starts on System. **Toggle theme** in the palette does the same.
- **Font size** — **A−** and **A+** step the whole interface smaller or larger, in five
  steps. Use it on a high-resolution display where text runs small. Unlike the theme, the
  font size is saved to your account and follows you to every browser you sign in on.

The same two settings, with a preview sentence, are on the **Appearance** card of the
**Settings** page, below **Members & roles**, where the font size reads as a percentage —
87.5%, 100%, 112.5%, 125% or 150%.

Animations follow your operating system's reduced-motion setting; there is no separate
switch in Ouroboros.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Open the full list from the account menu, **Keyboard shortcuts**.

| Where | Keys | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anywhere | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) | Open search |
| Anywhere | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close a menu, drawer or dialog |
| Search palette | <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move between actions, wrapping at the ends |
| Search palette | <kbd>↵</kbd> | Run the highlighted action |
| Sidebar | <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move between entries, wrapping at the ends |
| Sidebar | <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd> | Jump to the first or last entry |
| Sidebar | <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the entry |
| Account menu | <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move between items |
| Account menu | <kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> | Enter or leave the workspace submenu |
| Account menu | <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Activate the item |
| Account menu | <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Leave the menu |

Keyboard users can also jump past the sidebar and header with **Skip to content**, the
first thing <kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches on every page.

## What can go wrong

- **"That workspace could not be opened."** — switching workspace failed; try again, or
  sign out and choose the workspace on the sign-in page.
- **A screen shows less than you expected** — check the workspace chip: a focus
  repository narrows what you see. Choose **All repos** to see everything.
- **"Leave without saving?"** — you have unsaved changes in Settings. **Stay on this page**
  and save them, or **Discard changes and leave**.
- **The theme changed back** — the theme is saved per browser; set it again in each
  browser you use, or leave it on System.
- **Back returns you to the same place** — that is on purpose: Back and Forward restore
  how far you had scrolled.
