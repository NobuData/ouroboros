Finding. The docking gap is not sensors: our IMU and rangefinder match Skylink's published spec.[07] It is control — Skylink runs a wind-feedforward MPC in the final 2 m[12][31] while ours is PID with fixed gains (dock_ctrl.c:214, unchanged in 14 months).[git] Their abort logic retries from a re-planned approach vector; ours returns to loiter and waits for the operator — the behavior customers describe as "giving up."[19] Estimated closure: one epic, 5 tickets, ~3 weeks of loop time on the HIL rig.
